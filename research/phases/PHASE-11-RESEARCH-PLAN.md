**Sthiti: RESEARCH BAaki HAI.** Yeh file abhi research karne ki list hai. Neeche likhi baaton ko current feature ki verified jaankari na maanein jab tak official Google documentation check na ho.

## Kin topics par research karni hai?
1. **Removals tool:** Search results se URL ko kuch samay ke liye chhupane ki request, purane content ke requests aur request ki sthiti.
2. **Change of Address:** Website ko naye domain par shift karne par is tool ka use kab karna hai; redirects aur sitemap ki taiyari.
3. **Settings:** Website ownership verify karna, users ko access dena, permissions aur available settings.
4. **Property management:** Domain property aur URL-prefix property ka farq; property add/remove karna aur ownership verify karna.
5. **Data export aur API:** Kaunsa data mil sakta hai, login/authentication, usage limits aur restrictions.
6. Har feature ke practical examples aur aam galtiyan.
7. Google ke official links aur research check karne ki date.

## Phase complete kab hogi?
1. Har topic ko aasaan Hindi (Hinglish) mein samjhaya gaya ho.
2. Current features aur unki limits official documentation se verify ki gayi hon.
3. Temporary removal aur permanently search se hatane ka farq saaf ho.
4. Website shift karne mein redirects, canonical URLs, sitemap aur monitoring samjhaye gaye hon.
5. Live website ki checking ko general research se alag rakha gaya ho.
